## Objective

1. Completing a sales call (Submit Salescall) validates only:
   - Brands saved for the visit;
   - at least one Photo (unchanged region-type exemption: IB Team / OE Team
     exempt; no region type means required);
   - a Trade Request submitted by the same rep for the same sales call.
2. CRR Signage and Marketing no longer block completion and are removed from
   the footer "Requires:" checklist and the `canSubmitSalescall` logic.
3. Unchanged: Partially Completed / Cancelled paths, the Marketing
   CRR-before-create gate, and Trade Check availability (never required).
